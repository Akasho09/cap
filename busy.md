##  BUSY :
- Busy Infotech provides accounting and business management software solutions that help companies efficiently manage finances, taxation (especially GST), billing, inventory, and other business workflows — with features suitable for both small shops and larger enterprises. 
- Accounting and Inventory Software.
---------
- BUSY also offers an add-on named “Item Barcode Printing Software”. It is a software that works in conjunction with BUSY for printing Item Barcodes on any windows-compatible printer, thus obviating the need of costly conventional barcode printer. It picks up the Item Data either from BUSY or from an Excel Sheet.
