## LAWS OF MOTION :
- The Laws of Motion are formulated by Isaac Newton and form the foundation of classical mechanics. These laws explain the relationship between force, mass, and motion.

### 1️⃣ First Law of Motion (Law of Inertia)
- A body remains at rest or in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force.

#### 🧠 Key Concept: Inertia
- Inertia = Resistance to change in state of motion.
    - Depends on mass.
    - More mass → More inertia.
- ✨ Types of Inertia:
    - Inertia of rest
    - Inertia of motion
    - Inertia of direction

### 🔹 2️⃣ Second Law of Motion
- The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction of the force.
> F=ma
- Where:
    - F = Force
    - m = Mass
    - a = Acceleration

### 💡 Important Points:
> Momentum p=mv
- SI unit of force = Newton (N)
    - 1 N = force required to accelerate 1 kg mass by 1 m/s²

### 🔹 3️⃣ Third Law of Motion
- For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
- ⚖️ Important Notes:
    - Action and reaction:
    - Act on different bodies
    - Equal in magnitude
    - Opposite in direction

#### 🔹 Applications of Laws of Motion
| Situation          | Law Used   |
| ------------------ | ---------- |
| Wearing seatbelts  | First Law  |
| Kicking a football | Second Law |
| Rocket propulsion  | Third Law  |
| Recoil of gun      | Third Law  |
